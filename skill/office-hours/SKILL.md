# Office Hours

Run the Think stage. Ask six forcing questions: who is the user, what painful moment repeats, what is the
current workaround, what must change this week, what is explicitly out of scope, and how will the first value
be observed? Challenge the requested feature when the pain suggests a narrower or more valuable wedge.

Output a short approved brief at `.vemo/brief.md` or the project's product-doc location. Do not change source,
task state, hooks, or policy. Feed the brief into `plan-ceo-review`.
